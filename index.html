Designed for workflow purity.

Every detail is engineered to keep your workspace clean without changing how you fundamentally use macOS.

Zero Storage Footprint

Unlike other clipboard managers, Stoww does not use extra space to save your files or folders. It acts purely as a reference shelf. It remembers where your heavy 4K videos or project folders live without ever duplicating them on your hard drive.

Auto-Cleaning Retention

Files you don't need shouldn't stay on your shelf forever. Set Stoww to automatically forget files after 1 day, 1 week, or keep them indefinitely. It cleans up its own mess so you don't have to.

Drag In, Drag Out

Drag any file to the notch or the screen edge. Stoww catches it instantly. Later, simply open the Stoww window from your menu bar and drag those files exactly where you need to move them—be it a folder, an email, or Photoshop.

Native & Lightweight

Built from scratch in 100% native Swift and SwiftUI. It uses effectively zero CPU in the background, respects your battery life, and feels like a seamless part of the macOS ecosystem.

How to setup Stoww Pro

2

Click the Stoww icon in your Mac's menu bar, click the gear icon, and select "Upgrade to Stoww Pro".

3

Buy the License Key

Purchase your lifetime license to instantly receive a unique key via email.

4

Paste the Key to Activate

Paste your key into the app's upgrade window to instantly unlock unlimited Stows.

Flexible License Management

Your single license key can be activated on up to 2 separate Macs simultaneously (perfect for your iMac and MacBook).

Moving to a new Mac? It's easy. You can deactivate a machine at any time to free up a slot:

  • From the App: Click "Deactivate License" inside Stoww's settings menu.
  • From the Web: Click the "Manage Order" link in your original email receipt to remotely deactivate any offline machine.
